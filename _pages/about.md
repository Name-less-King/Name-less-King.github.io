I am a fourth-year Ph.D. student at the [School of Data Science](https://sds.fudan.edu.cn), [Fudan University](https://www.fudan.edu.cn), advised by [Prof. L. Jeff Hong](https://jeffhongliu.github.io). My research develops probabilistic and statistical methods for reliable decision-making under uncertainty.

I collaborate closely with [Dr. Qingkai Zhang](https://scholars.cityu.edu.hk/en/persons/qzhang793/), [Prof. Nifei Lin](https://sime.sufe.edu.cn/lnf/main.htm), [Prof. Jiaye Teng](https://www.tengjiaye.com), and [Prof. Hao Zeng](https://zenghao-stat.github.io).

## Research Interests

I am particularly interested in:

* Uncertainty Quantification (e.g., Conformal Prediction)
* Generative Modeling
* Risk Management
